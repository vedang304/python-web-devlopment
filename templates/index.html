ABOUT SKILLLOOP

Learn Together. Grow Together.

SkillLoop is a student-focused platform where students can exchange skills and learn from each other.

Instead of paying for every skill they want to learn, students can share their existing knowledge and learn from someone who has the skill they need.

With assessments, ratings, skill points and rankings, SkillLoop makes peer learning measurable and engaging.
